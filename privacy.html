Privacy notice.

Updated November 13, 2026 · Current development build

Virus Bloom: Block Puzzle is developed by LNTT Apps. This notice describes the game’s current features and this website. The game is in development and is not yet publicly released.

Progress and game accounts

The game saves progress, best move counts, settings and pending updates on your device. Our LNTT backend uses an app-specific guest identifier or verified platform account identifier to provide online rankings, progress syncing and a Diamond/item wallet. Account providers manage their own sign-in credentials.

Public rankings may display your gamer name, game results and avatar. Game Center photos may be stored as a small thumbnail for rankings. Optional friends features use platform friend identifiers when you allow access. We do not display account email addresses as ranking names.

Optional gameplay analytics

Firebase Analytics is off until you choose “Allow analytics.” After consent, it may receive app/session/device information and gameplay events such as starting or finishing a round and using an item. Our custom gameplay events do not include your gamer name, account identifier, credentials or purchase receipts.

You can turn Usage analytics off in Settings. Advertising personalization and cross-app advertising tracking are disabled. Ads are not enabled in the current game.

Diamonds and purchases

The backend records wallet balances, daily claims, item use and transaction identifiers to deliver purchases and avoid duplicate rewards. Apple processes iOS purchases; we do not receive payment-card details. Current purchase testing uses the sandbox environment; production purchases remain disabled.

Optional reminders

You can enable a local reminder for approximately 24 hours after leaving the game, subject to device notification permission and system scheduling. This is a notification scheduled on your device, not a remote marketing push service. Returning to the game cancels the pending reminder. You can turn reminders off in Settings.

Providers and this website

Game services use Google Cloud and Firestore. Optional analytics uses Google Firebase. Apple and Google provide their respective platform account services. This static website is hosted with GitHub Pages. It does not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts or cookies; the hosting provider may process technical request data to serve and secure the site.

Deletion and retention

You can request deletion of your game account in the app or contact support. Deletion removes game progress, ranking and wallet records. Limited deletion and transaction replay markers may remain to prevent a deleted account or processed purchase from being recreated or credited again. Platform account and store records remain under the relevant provider’s control. We retain information while needed to provide these services and maintain transaction integrity.

How to delete game data or send a request ↗

Contact

For questions about access, correction or deletion, email lnttapps@gmail.com. We may need to verify ownership before responding to an account request. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or payment-card details.
