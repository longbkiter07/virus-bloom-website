THE CURE IS IN THE CONNECTION

Room for a block.
Room for a brilliant move.

Choose any of the three medicines in your tray. Rotate it. Find its spot. Fill a row, column, or marked 3 × 3 region with one matching color to clear it.

01

Pick your medicine

Three pieces, any order. Drag one onto the board and use rotation to find a fit.

02

Bring nine together

Complete all nine cells of a row, column, or marked region in the same color. Wildcards help complete a match.

03

Give viruses the boot

Clear every virus to win. Keep room for your next move—when none of your medicines can fit, the round ends.
